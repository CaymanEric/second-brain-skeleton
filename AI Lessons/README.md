# AI Lessons

The AI hub. One markdown summary per video or course lesson, about 435 notes.
`AI Lessons.md` is the subject view across every source; `YouTube Lessons.md`
is the index of videos and the guidelines a summary has to follow.

Folders are by source, not subject. YouTube notes live here, reel notes in
`Reels/`, newsletter notes in `Mail/`. The two creators with the most notes
have their own subfolders; everyone else sits at the top level.

## Summary format

Complete substance, brief style. URL, channel, and timestamps per section.
Commands and version numbers come from the on-screen text, never from the
audio. Every summary must pull at least one reusable idea into `Atlas/` and be
linked from the index, so the graph chain holds: Home > index > summary > Atlas.

Notes are named `<Creator> - <Short Title>.md`.
